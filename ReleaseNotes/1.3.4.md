# Parrot 1.3.4

- Replaces the start beep with a softer, lower-pitched wooden tap. It has a short attack and a quick, gentle decay, and lasts just 55 milliseconds.
- Recording still starts before the sound plays, with no added wait, trimming or audio filtering. Start and stop sound preferences are preserved.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Includes Apple silicon and Intel binaries. Keeps the existing signing identity. This release is Apple Development signed and is not Apple-notarized.
