# Parrot 1.3.2

- Fixes clipped opening words caused by waiting for the start sound and its echo gap before microphone capture. Recording now starts immediately by default, including when upgrading with recording sounds enabled.
- Separates start and stop sounds in General settings. The stop sound keeps its previous setting and plays after the microphone closes. The optional start sound is off by default and explicitly labelled as delaying recording; enabling it retains echo protection.
- Clarifies that Playback delay changes the wait before listening to a completed take, not when recording starts.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Includes Apple silicon and Intel binaries. Keeps the signing identity used by 1.3.0 and 1.3.1. This release is Apple Development signed and is not Apple-notarized.
