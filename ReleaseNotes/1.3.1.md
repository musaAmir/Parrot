# Parrot 1.3.1

- Centers recording and playback panels within the active display’s usable area, including displays with different screen origins.
- Fixes sideways movement when recording buttons are shown or hidden. The visible panel and its window now use matching bounds.
- Adds a drag grip to both panels and remembers their positions separately between sessions. Panels stay reachable when displays change.
- Redesigns playback to match the recording panel, with consistent materials, spacing and buttons, a seek slider and elapsed/total time.
- Adds a playback close button that stops playback and dismisses the panel immediately.
- Makes the app’s Settings menu command open the full settings window.
- Adds a shared Squared/Rounded shape setting, previews of both panels, and Reset Panel Positions under Appearance.
- Prevents closing an old playback panel from interrupting the start cue of a new recording.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Includes Apple silicon and Intel binaries. This release keeps the signing identity used by 1.3.0.

Validation includes placement and preference regression tests, live dragging and position restoration, centered control resizing, both shape styles, playback dismissal, recording/cue regressions, universal builds and signed Sparkle update checks. Intel is cross-compiled; physical Intel hardware validation remains outstanding.
