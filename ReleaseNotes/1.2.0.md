# Parrot 1.2.0

- Adds Sparkle update checks in the menu bar and Settings, with signed update archives and a signed feed. Automatic downloads and installation are off by default.
- Fixes hold shortcuts failing to stop when modifier keys are released first.
- Cancels delayed playback when another take or explicit playback action supersedes it.
- Gives every recording a unique filename and prunes recent takes throughout the session.
- Uses per-player output selection so playback no longer changes the system output device.
- Stops recording and playback on sleep, preserves user input-device changes, and safely cancels the recorder at quit.
- Releases the recording overlay’s SwiftUI content while hidden and avoids shortcut-setting updates for every microphone meter tick.
- Shows audio failures in Settings, clamps invalid saved preferences, and fixes saving a recording over its own source file.
- Includes the missing app executable metadata and universal Apple silicon/Intel binaries.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Microphone and Accessibility permissions are needed for recording and global shortcuts.

**Installation:** Unzip Parrot-1.2.0.zip, quit the old version, and move Parrot.app to Applications. Versions before 1.2.0 need this one manual download to gain the updater. Future releases can update through Parrot.

**Signing status:** This build is ad-hoc signed and has not been notarized by Apple. macOS may block first launch. You may need to re-approve microphone or Accessibility access after replacing an ad-hoc build. Sparkle signatures verify the update archive and feed; they do not replace Apple notarization.

Automated checks cover recording-file retention, shortcut release tracking, delayed-playback cancellation, invalid settings, failed playback, update configuration, release metadata, and signature tampering. Microphone hardware, Accessibility approval, and device-switch behavior still need hands-on validation on supported Macs; cross-compilation does not verify Intel hardware behavior.
