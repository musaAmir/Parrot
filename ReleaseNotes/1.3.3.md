# Parrot 1.3.3

- Replaces the long start sound with a soft, 55-millisecond tick.
- Starts microphone capture before playing the tick. Removes the sound-completion wait and echo gap entirely, so enabling start feedback never adds a recording delay.
- Keeps the recorded audio unfiltered and untrimmed. A speaker cue may be picked up by the microphone.
- Keeps independent start and stop sound settings under General. The stop sound still plays after capture closes. A failed recording never plays the start cue.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Includes Apple silicon and Intel binaries. Keeps the signing identity used by 1.3.0–1.3.2. This release is Apple Development signed and is not Apple-notarized.
