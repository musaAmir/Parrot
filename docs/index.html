How it works

Four seconds, start to finish

0.0 s

Hold ⌘ ⇧ Z

Works in any app, at any time — you never switch windows or click record. Rebind it to whatever you like.

0.0 s

Speak

A small indicator shows your actual input level, so you can see the mic is live and you aren't clipping.

+0.5 s

Release, and wait a beat

A short gap before playback, so you aren't hearing yourself over yourself. Adjustable from 0 to 5 seconds.

+0.5 s

Listen

Playback with scrub, pause and replay. Play it back as many times as you want, then move on.

What it's good for

Your ears lie to you

You hear your own voice through bone as well as air, which is why recordings always sound wrong at first. The fix is repetition — hear the recording often enough and you start hearing what's actually there.

Pronunciation drilling

Say the phrase, hear it back, adjust. The loop is fast enough to run twenty times without losing patience.

Presentation practice

Catch the filler words, the trailing sentences and the pace you can't hear while you're talking.

Interview prep

Answer out loud, play it back, and hear whether you actually answered the question.

Accent work

Record the same sentence before and after a correction, and hear whether anything really changed.

A quick mic check

Confirm the right input is live before a call, without joining one to find out.

Singing and pitch

Hold, sing a phrase, and listen back against the note you were aiming for.

Details

Small on purpose

Parrot does one thing. It has no account, no network code and no telemetry — the app has no way to talk to the internet at all. Recordings stay on your Mac until you delete them.
